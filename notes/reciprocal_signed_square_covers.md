# Reciprocal signed-square covers

This note gives the exact quotient formulation of the remaining arithmetic
conditions.  It is a necessary reduction, not a rational-point
classification.

## The elliptic base and its two invariant functions

Let `C` be the normalization of the two-root factorization curve
`R1=R0=0`.  Its reciprocal involution is

```text
tau(s,a,b) = (1/s,a/b,1/b).
```

On the genus-one quotient `E=C/<tau>` define

```text
U=s+1/s,  W=b+1/b.
```

Eliminating `a`, then adjoining the reciprocal quadratics for `b` and `s`,
gives the exact perfect-square identity

```text
Res_s(Res_b(Res_a(R1,R0),b^2-W*b+1),s^2-U*s+1)=K(U,W)^2.
```

The irreducible polynomial `K` has bidegree `(20,10)` and 150 terms.  Its
canonical coefficient list is `artifacts/reciprocal_b_quotient.json`; the
identity is regenerated by
`research/symbolic_algebra/reciprocal_b_quotient.py`.

For generic `U`, the degree-10 cover `E -> P1_U` has ten points: the twenty
points above the two reciprocal values of `s` are paired by `tau`.  Since
`K` is irreducible and has degree 10 in `W`, the induced map from `E` to the
normalization of `K=0` has degree one.  Thus `K` is a birational plane model
of the same elliptic quotient, not a further unidentified quotient.

The implication is one-way until the stored factor data are reconstructed:
a rational point on `K=0` need not lift to rational `s,a,b`.

## All three square characters

The exact identities

```text
U+2 = (s+1)^2/s,
U-2 = (s-1)^2/s,
W+2 = (b+1)^2/b,
2-W = -(b-1)^2/b
```

show that a genuine factor with `s=z^2` and `b=+c^2` lies on the
`(Z/2)^3` cover obtained by adjoining square roots of

```text
U+2, U-2, W+2.
```

For `b=-c^2`, the full cover may replace `-V` by `2-W`, but this is a
basis change rather than a literal equality of square classes on `E`; the
precise relation is proved below.  Both `U+2` and `U-2` are required: if they
have rational square roots `v,r`, then
`z=(v+r)/2` and `1/z=(v-r)/2`.  Imposing only `U+2` gives a useful necessary
genus-4 quotient but is not equivalent to the square-parameter condition.

## Exact branch table

The local Newton data at `s=0,+/-1,infinity` give the following odd branch
divisors on `E`.

| character | `s=0/infinity` | `s=1` | `s=-1` | total |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| `U+2` | 4 | 0 | 2 | 6 |
| `U-2` | 4 | 10 | 0 | 14 |
| `W+2` | 2 | 4 mixed pairs | 0 | 6 |
| `2-W` | 2 | 6 negative pairs | 2 | 10 |

At `s=1`, the root near `+1` has slope `5`; the other slopes are the four
distinct roots of

```text
c^4-8*c^2-12*c-4.
```

Its discriminant is `-11008`, its value at `5` is `361`, and its gcd with
the polynomial obtained by `c -> -c` is one.  These identities prove that
`b+1` is simple on the four mixed pairs and `b-1` is simple on the six
negative-negative pairs.  At `s=-1`, the initial form `y^5+64d` shows that
no two leading fifth-root coefficients sum to zero.  The complete exact
audit is `research/arithmetic_geometry/reciprocal_b_branch_audit.py`.

Riemann--Hurwitz gives the seven character-quotient genera

```text
b positive: 4, 8, 4, 7, 5, 5, 6,
b negative: 4, 8, 6, 7, 5, 5, 4.
```

For the full `(Z/2)^3` covers, Kani--Rosen (equivalently direct
Riemann--Hurwitz) gives genus `33` in either sign.  The smaller `V4` covers
formed from `U+2` and the signed-`b` character have genera `11` and `13`;
they remain rigorous necessary sieves.

The change to the lower-degree `q` coordinate, all seven labeled branch
supports, the compact open model, and the generic inverse formulas are
exported by
`research/arithmetic_geometry/new_three_square_character_covers.py`.

## Negative-b basis correction

The tempting identity

```text
(-V)/(2-W) = (a/(b-1))^2
```

holds on the factor curve before quotienting.  It does **not** say that
`[-V]=[2-W]` on `E`: under `(a,b) -> (a/b,1/b)` the displayed square root
changes sign, so it is not a function on the quotient.

In the `T,B,A` chart,

```text
q = T^2+T^(-2),
V = A^2/(1-B^2),
W = 2*(1+B^2)/(1-B^2).
```

The function

```text
R = A*T/(2*B*(T^2+1))
```

is invariant under both deck transformations
`(T,B,A)->(-T,-B,A)` and `T->1/T`, and exact cancellation gives

```text
R^2 = (-V)/((q+2)*(2-W)).
```

Therefore the correct relation in `Q(E)^*/Q(E)^{*2}` is

```text
[-V] = [q+2] + [2-W].
```

Since the full negative cover already adjoins `sqrt(q+2)`, its chosen basis
`(q+2,q-2,2-W)` defines exactly the same full cover as the physical basis
`(q+2,q-2,-V)`.  For individual character quotients, however, the labels
must be converted by

```text
(a,b,c)_physical -> (a+c,b,c)_chosen.
```

In particular, literal `-V` is chosen-basis character `101`, not `001`.
The corrected labels and this basis conversion are recorded in
`research/arithmetic_geometry/new_three_square_character_covers.json`.

## All seven literal characters are ramified

Write `Zb`, `Zn`, `L`, `M`, and `N` for the disjoint atomic normalization
sets in the branch certificate, of degrees `2,2,2,4,6`.  The global
identities

```text
(q-2)/(U-2) = (q-2)^2/16,
(q+2)/((U+2)*(U-2)) = (q-2)^2/64,
V/(W+2) = (A/2)^2
```

include zeros and poles, not merely affine intersections.  They give

```text
odd(q+2) = L + M + N,
odd(q-2) = Zb + Zn + M + N,
odd(V)   = Zb + M.
```

Symmetric difference then gives the following odd-divisor degrees for the
literal basis `(q+2,q-2,epsilon*V)`; multiplying by the constant `-1` does
not change a divisor.

| bits | odd-divisor degree |
|---|---:|
| `001` | 6 |
| `010` | 14 |
| `011` | 8 |
| `100` | 12 |
| `101` | 10 |
| `110` | 6 |
| `111` | 8 |

Every nonzero combination is therefore ramified.  Equivalently, the span of
the three cuboid characters meets the subgroup of square classes with even
divisor only in the identity.  This statement is on the normalization of
`H` itself and does not assume the still-open identification C025.

Conditionally on an eventual identification with

```text
E1: y^2=x^3-5*x+4,
```

put `X=x-1`.  Then `div(X)=2*(T)-2*(O)`, so the standard 2-isogeny Kummer
class is unramified.  It cannot be a constant times a nontrivial product of
the three ramified characters modulo squares.  This rules out a direct
function-field Kummer identity; it does not rule out a later arithmetic
argument using explicit transport and local evaluations.

The lightweight exact regression is
`research/arithmetic_geometry/character_kummer_obstruction.py`, with saved
certificate `character_kummer_obstruction.json`.

## What would finish the proof

It is enough to determine the rational points on the seven lower-genus
character quotients, combine their compatible images on `E(Q)`, and perform
the exact reverse lifts through `s^2-U*s+1`, `b^2-W*b+1`, and `R1=R0=0`.
The still-missing certificate is an explicit Weierstrass model of `E`,
certified Mordell--Weil data, and a complete covering/Mordell--Weil sieve for
the character covers.  Genus computations or bounded searches alone do not
supply that certificate.
